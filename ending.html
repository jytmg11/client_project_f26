Ending the Experience

Complete and Transition the Work

Ending an engaged learning experience includes completing final deliverables, organizing files, explaining next steps, and making sure partners can use or continue the work. Students should plan the transition before the final deadline.

Close Relationships Respectfully

Students should communicate results, thank partners, ask for feedback, and close the relationship in a professional way. A thoughtful ending helps protect trust between the university, students, and community partners.

Reflect on Learning and Impact

Reflection helps students explain what they learned and how the experience shaped their skills. Students can use this reflection for resumes, portfolios, interviews, and future engaged learning opportunities.
