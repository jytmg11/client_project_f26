During Your Experience

Manage the Project and Team

During an engaged learning experience, students need clear project plans, regular check-ins, and shared expectations. Useful resources for this stage include project planning worksheets, team agreements, meeting agendas, and progress tracking checklists.

Communicate With Partners

Strong communication helps students build trust with partners and respond to changes during a project. Students should practice writing clear updates, asking useful questions, documenting decisions, and following up after meetings.

Navigate Challenges

Project work can include conflict, shifting timelines, unclear expectations, or unexpected technical limits. Students should ask for help early, document concerns, and use feedback as part of the learning process.

Suggested Resources

  • Weekly project check-in template
  • Team conflict reflection worksheet
  • Guide to giving and receiving feedback
  • Ethical engagement checklist
